About the Master Suite

Designed specifically for professional land surveyors, this powerful suite includes 13 integrated AutoLISP commands built to handle complex area calculations, Tippon drawing, coordinates tables, and dual-unit dimensioning instantly inside AutoCAD.

Included 13 Professional Commands:

1. CTABLE: Survey Coordinates Table Generator
2. LGTABLE: Auto Numbered Legend Table & Hatch
3. ACGT: Area Labels in Acres & Gunthas
4. CHKAREA: Detailed Area Dialog Box & Prompt
5. SQAREA: Area in Sq.Meters & Sq.Feet at Center
6. DALL: Dual Unit Dimensions (Meters & Feet)
7. LMF2: Dual Align Labels on Lines
8. PDALL: Auto Polyline Dimensions
9. MF: Below Line Label (Meters & Feet-Inches)
10. RAM: Below Line Label (Rupees-Annas & Meters)
11. FT: Below Line Label (Only Feet & Inches)
12. TFC: Tippon Fix, Connect (With Auto Text Update)
13. TIPPON: Numpad Directional Drawing Suite
